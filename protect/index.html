Protect PDF — questions

What does password protection do?

The PDF becomes unreadable without the password — anyone who opens it is asked for the password first. Optionally, printing and copying can also be restricted.

What if I forget the password?

There is no way to recover or remove a forgotten password — that's the whole point of encryption. Write it down somewhere safe before downloading.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. The encryption happens entirely in your browser — your PDF and your password never leave your device.
