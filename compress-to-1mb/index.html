      How do we keep your files private?

      Do you upload my files to a server?

      Never. PDF Slim runs entirely in your browser — pages are rendered and re-encoded on your own device. Your files don't touch our servers for even a second.

      How can I verify that myself?

      Press F12 to open Developer Tools, click the Network tab, then compress a PDF. You'll see zero uploads. In fact, you can disconnect from the internet and compression still works.

      Do you track me or use cookies?

      No cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing stored about you — the only thing saved is a local counter of bytes processed on your own device.

      Why compress to 1MB?

      Why exactly 1MB?

      “Max 1MB” is the most common upload cap on the internet — from email-era attachment limits to modern SaaS and CRM forms. At 1MB, even photo-heavy PDFs keep near-original clarity.

      Will my document still look good?

      The default answer when a portal just says “maximum 1 MB” — which is a lot of them.
