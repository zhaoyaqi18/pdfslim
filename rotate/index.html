Rotate PDF — questions

What rotation angles are available?

90° clockwise, 180° (upside down), and 90° counter-clockwise — enough to fix any sideways or flipped page in one click.

Will rotating affect my file's quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation — the content and quality stay exactly the same, with no re-rendering.

Can I rotate pages individually?

This tool rotates every page in the document at once — perfect for fixing a whole scan that was saved the wrong way.
