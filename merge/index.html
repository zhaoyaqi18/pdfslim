    Merge PDF — questions

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    As many as you like — there's no limit. Drop two files or twenty, reorder them with the arrows, and merge them all into one PDF in a single pass.

    Can I change the order of files before merging?

    Yes. Each file in the list has up/down arrows, so you can arrange them exactly as you want them to appear in the final document.

    Does merging reduce the quality of my PDFs?

    No. Merging combines the original pages as-is — nothing is re-encoded or compressed, so every page keeps its original quality and resolution.
